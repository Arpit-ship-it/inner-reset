Clarity before you begin

Frequently asked questions.

Are sessions conducted online or offline?
+
Life Coaching and Healing sessions are conducted online. POSH consulting can be online or offline depending on organisational requirements.
Who can benefit from healing sessions?
+
Individuals dealing with stress, emotional overwhelm, relationship challenges, health-related stress, career pressure, or money anxiety.
Is healing a medical treatment?
+
No. Healing sessions are non-medical and do not replace professional medical or psychological care. They work alongside personal awareness and responsibility.
Is POSH consulting offered across India?
+
Yes. POSH consulting services - including External Member support, training, and awareness sessions - are available across India.
How is confidentiality maintained?
+
Individual sessions are private and confidential. POSH engagements follow statutory confidentiality, neutrality, and process requirements.
